Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, file a complaint, or communicate with us. This includes:

  • Personal Identifiers: Name, Aadhaar number, phone number, and email address.
  • Grievance Data: Description of the issue, category, and geographical location (GPS coordinates).
  • Media: Photographs uploaded to support your grievance reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To process and resolve your civic complaints.
  • To communicate status updates and resolution notifications.
  • To analyze municipal trends and improve urban infrastructure.
  • To prevent fraudulent or duplicate reporting.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Your data is shared only with the relevant Municipal Government Departments (e.g., Road, Water, Electricity) responsible for resolving the reported issue. We do not sell or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. Access to your Aadhaar and contact details is restricted to authorized administrative personnel only.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access your reported history and update your contact information through the Citizen Portal. For account deletion or data portability requests, please contact our privacy officer.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@fixmycity.gov.in.
