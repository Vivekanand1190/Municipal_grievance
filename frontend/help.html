How can we help?

Find answers to common questions about using the FixMyCity platform.

How do I file a new complaint?
Log in to the Citizen Portal using your Aadhaar and Phone number. Click on the "File Complaint" tab, upload a photo of the issue, select the category, and provide a brief description. Your GPS location will be automatically captured from the photo metadata.
What is "Duplicate Detection"?
Our system automatically checks if a similar issue has already been reported nearby. If a match is found, you can "Support" the existing report instead of creating a new one. This helps the government prioritize issues with higher community support.
How long does it take to resolve an issue?
Resolution times vary depending on the complexity of the issue and the department's workload. You can track the real-time status (Pending, In Progress, Resolved) through your "History" tab.
What if my issue is rejected?
If an issue is rejected, a reason will be provided by the department officer. Common reasons include "Out of Jurisdiction" or "Insufficient Information." You can resubmit with more details if necessary.
